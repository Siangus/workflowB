## Purpose
Grow the minimum viable product and its supporting design together, avoiding both infrastructure-first and feature-polish-first waste.

## Trigger
A product needs architectural investment while value delivery is urgent.

## Procedure
1. Identify the smallest useful versions of the key features.
2. Build only the enabling foundation needed by the next slices.
3. Refine feature capability and foundation in successive iterations.
4. Inspect the running product and adjust investment.

## Checks
- Foundation work has a named feature or risk it enables.
- The product remains valuable and shippable throughout.

## Anti-patterns
- Completing architecture before exposing any user value.
