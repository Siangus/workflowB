## Purpose
Maintain links from business objectives and rules through requirements, design, code, tests, releases, and changes.

## Trigger
The project is high-risk, regulated, long-lived, multi-team, or needs reliable change impact analysis.

## Procedure
1. Choose traceability depth based on risk and cost.
2. Define link types and owners.
3. Create links from requirements to source, design, implementation, and verification artifacts.
4. Use links during impact analysis and release readiness.
5. Audit stale or missing links periodically.

## Checks
- Every high-risk or regulated requirement has implementation and verification evidence.
- Links support an actual impact or coverage query.

## Anti-patterns
- Creating a large trace matrix that nobody uses to make decisions.
