## Purpose
Protect business transactions from lost updates, inconsistent reads, deadlocks, and cross-session conflicts.

## Trigger
Multiple requests can update the same business data or a business transaction spans separate system transactions.

## Procedure
1. Identify business and system transaction boundaries.
2. Choose isolation, immutability, optimistic or pessimistic locking, or coarse-grained locking.
3. Define conflict detection and user/system recovery.
4. Test contention, rollback, retry, and deadlock behavior.

## Checks
- Concurrent updates cannot silently overwrite valid changes.
- Conflict outcomes are deterministic and actionable.

## Anti-patterns
- Using a lock without defining its scope and failure behavior.
- Confusing database transaction success with business transaction completion.
