## Purpose
Ensure requirements come from relevant user classes, customers, sponsors, and other affected stakeholders.

## Trigger
A project has multiple user roles, indirect users, or conflicting viewpoints.

## Procedure
1. Identify stakeholder and user classes.
2. Describe their characteristics, goals, authority, and availability.
3. Select representative participants for elicitation and review.
4. Resolve representation gaps before baselining.

## Checks
- Every significant user class has a known representative or documented gap.
- Decision authority is explicit.

## Anti-patterns
- Assuming a sponsor or developer speaks for all users.
