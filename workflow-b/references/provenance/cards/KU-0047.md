## Purpose
Control the cost and blast radius of expected changes through responsibility, coupling, cohesion, indirection, and deferred binding.

## Trigger
The system must absorb new requirements, replace dependencies, or support multiple variants.

## Procedure
1. List likely change sources and affected responsibilities.
2. Localize likely changes behind interfaces or encapsulation.
3. Reduce unnecessary coupling and dependency distance.
4. Evaluate the change scenario against the current architecture.
5. Track architecture debt when a short-term shortcut increases future cost.

## Checks
- A likely change has a bounded impact area.
- The architecture exposes where change work will occur.

## Anti-patterns
- Adding abstractions without a change scenario.
- Allowing cross-layer or cross-context shortcuts.
