# KU-0016: Core, supporting, and generic subdomains

## Purpose
Focus scarce expert effort on the domain that differentiates the business.

## Trigger
Deciding where to invest custom design, product ownership, or specialist expertise.

## Procedure
1. Inventory subdomains.
2. Classify each as core, supporting, or generic.
3. Allocate strongest people and bespoke investment to the core domain.
4. Buy, reuse, or simplify generic concerns when prudent.

## Checks
- Core-domain rationale is explicit.
- Investment level matches strategic importance.

## Anti-patterns
- Applying equal bespoke-design effort to every subsystem.
