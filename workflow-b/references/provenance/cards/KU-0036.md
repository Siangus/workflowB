## Purpose
Allocate constrained delivery capacity by making trade-offs visible across value, cost, risk, and urgency.

## Trigger
Selecting a release, iteration, or change request.

## Procedure
1. Define the decision criteria with stakeholders.
2. Estimate relative value, cost, risk, and dependency.
3. Apply a suitable technique such as pairwise comparison, three-level grouping, or weighted scoring.
4. Assign selected work to a release or iteration.
5. Revisit priority when evidence or constraints change.

## Checks
- Priority has a recorded rationale.
- The team can explain what was deferred and why.

## Anti-patterns
- Declaring every requested item highest priority.
