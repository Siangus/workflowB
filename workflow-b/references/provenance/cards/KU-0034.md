## Purpose
Turn quality attributes into ranked, measurable, testable requirements with explicit trade-offs.

## Trigger
A system has performance, reliability, security, usability, interoperability, scalability, or other quality concerns.

## Procedure
1. Start with a broad attribute taxonomy.
2. Trim to relevant attributes and rank their importance.
3. Elicit concrete scenarios and measures for each priority attribute.
4. Write measurable quality requirements and constraints.
5. Analyze trade-offs and trace them to architecture and tests.

## Checks
- Every priority quality attribute has a measurable condition and verification method.
- Conflicting attributes have a recorded trade-off decision.

## Anti-patterns
- Writing 'fast', 'secure', or 'user friendly' without a measurable context.
