Keep engineering work value-centered, simple, clear, change-aware, and reusable.

Trigger: Making scope, design, implementation, or review trade-offs.

Inputs
- Goals
- Constraints
- User evidence

Outputs
- Decision records
- Design choices

Procedure
1. Clarify value.
2. Prefer simplest credible design.
3. Make intent explicit.
4. Plan for likely change.
5. Reuse proven patterns when cheaper.

Checks
- Decisions show value and risk.

Anti-patterns
- Confusing novelty with value.
