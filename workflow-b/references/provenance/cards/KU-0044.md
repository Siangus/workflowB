## Purpose
Convert vague quality goals into stimuli, environments, responses, and measurable response targets.

## Trigger
A requirement says fast, secure, available, modifiable, testable, or deployable without measurable context.

## Procedure
1. Name the source of the stimulus.
2. Describe the stimulus and operating environment.
3. Describe the expected system response.
4. Define a measurable response target.
5. Trace the scenario to architecture tactics and tests.

## Checks
- Scenario is observable and measurable.
- Architecture and test evidence address the same scenario.

## Anti-patterns
- Using adjectives such as fast or reliable without conditions.
- Leaving quality attributes as a late testing concern.
