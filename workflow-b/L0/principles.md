# Invariant Principles

1. Start from a measurable problem, scope boundary, and accountable decision owner.
2. Requirements are owned, prioritized, testable, linked to their rationale, and distinguish confirmed facts from proposals, assumptions, and open questions.
3. Architecture decisions are driven by quality scenarios and change forces.
4. A completed increment is integrated, verified, and potentially releasable.
5. Interfaces expose stable external behavior, not accidental implementation details.
6. Security, reliability, performance, consistency, and observability are checked across states.
7. Preserve version identity, change history, impact analysis, and acceptance evidence.
8. Use the smallest process and artifact weight that satisfies project risk and obligations.
9. Feed test, release, and production evidence back into requirements, risks, and planning.
10. Construction begins only after a project design package has passed active review for evidence and implementation detail; an agent cannot approve its own package.
