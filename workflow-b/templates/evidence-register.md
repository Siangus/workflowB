# Evidence Register

Use one row for each material fact, interpretation, proposal, assumption, or open question. Do not turn a row into a baseline requirement or design decision until its class permits it.

| Evidence ID | Claim | Class | Source Type | Locator / Meeting Record | Captured On | Owner | Confidence / Reasoning | Affected Artifacts | Validation or Decision Needed By | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Blocking Rules

- A `Confirmed fact` must identify a source, locator or meeting record, date, and accountable owner.
- A `Derived interpretation` must link to the evidence IDs from which it follows and state the reasoning.
- An `Assumption` must state its impact if false, validation method, owner, and deadline.
- An `Open question` must state whether it blocks construction, who decides, and what answer is needed.
- A book, framework, or reference codebase may support a method or option; it is not product evidence.
