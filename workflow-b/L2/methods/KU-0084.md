# KU-0084: Adaptive architecture and feedback

**Type:** `principle`
**Lifecycle:** `maintenance`
**Trigger:** The system or its environment changes faster than initial assumptions remain valid.

## Purpose
Evolve people, process, architecture, and information flow together as demand, scale, and failure evidence change.

## Procedure
1. Observe demand, incidents, capacity, and change cost.
2. Identify which architectural or process assumption is now limiting.
3. Make a small reversible improvement.
4. Measure the result and feed it into the next decision.

## Checks
- Adaptation is driven by evidence.
- Architecture and operational changes have owners and follow-up measures.
