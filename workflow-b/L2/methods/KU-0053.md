# KU-0053: Architecture evaluation and ATAM

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** Competing architectures or high-impact quality trade-offs require a decision.

## Purpose
Reduce architecture risk before implementation by examining scenarios, tactics, trade-offs, and sensitivity points with stakeholders.

## Procedure
1. Collect business drivers and quality scenarios.
2. Present the architecture and identify approaches.
3. Analyze scenarios to expose sensitivity points, trade-offs, and risks.
4. Record findings, priorities, and recommended actions.
5. Use a lightweight evaluation when full ATAM cost is not justified.

## Checks
- Stakeholders participate in the evaluation.
- Trade-offs and risks are recorded, not merely discussed.
- Actions have owners and follow-up evidence.
