# KU-0037: Requirements validation through review and acceptance criteria

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `verification`
**Trigger:** A requirement set is ready for baseline, iteration commitment, or implementation.

## Purpose
Validate requirements before costly implementation by reviewing them with customers, developers, and testers and deriving acceptance evidence.

## Procedure
1. Prepare requirements, models, and acceptance conditions.
2. Inspect for correctness, completeness, consistency, feasibility, necessity, and testability.
3. Review with representative stakeholders, developers, and testers.
4. Resolve findings or record a risk decision.
5. Approve the validated subset.

## Checks
- Every accepted requirement has acceptance evidence.
- Unresolved disputes have a decision owner and date.
