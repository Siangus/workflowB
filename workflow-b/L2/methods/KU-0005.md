# KU-0005: Risk management

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `risk`
**Trigger:** Project start or high-risk choice.

## Purpose
Identify, rank, mitigate, monitor, and escalate uncertain conditions.

## Procedure
1. Identify risks.
2. Estimate probability/impact.
3. Rank exposure.
4. Define mitigation and trigger.
5. Review at gates.

## Checks
- High exposure has owner and trigger.
