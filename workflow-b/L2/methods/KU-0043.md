# KU-0043: Architecture as a quality-and-change decision structure

**Type:** `principle`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** A requirement, technology choice, or organizational constraint can affect multiple quality attributes or future change cost.

## Purpose
Use architecture to make early decisions that enable required quality attributes and constrain expensive changes.

## Procedure
1. Identify the externally visible structures and decisions.
2. Connect each decision to quality attributes, stakeholders, constraints, and likely changes.
3. Make the smallest set of decisions that controls the important risks.
4. Record the decision and its consequences.

## Checks
- Each important decision has a quality or change rationale.
- Stakeholders can identify what the architecture constrains.
