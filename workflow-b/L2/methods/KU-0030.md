# KU-0030: Collaborative requirements elicitation

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `requirements`
**Trigger:** Requirements are incomplete, disputed, or based on assumptions.

## Purpose
Discover needs through structured conversations, workshops, scenarios, document analysis, and interface analysis.

## Procedure
1. Prepare goals, scope, participants, and questions.
2. Use interviews, workshops, observation, document analysis, or interface analysis.
3. Elicit scenarios, exceptions, constraints, and business rules.
4. Record decisions, open issues, and follow-up owners.
5. Review findings with participants.

## Checks
- Each session has a goal and output.
- Open issues have owners and dates.
