# KU-0024: Potentially shippable vertical increments

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `construction`
**Trigger:** Planning or reporting work inside a short delivery cadence.

## Purpose
Treat a feature as done only when its requirements, design, implementation, and verification form a working product slice.

## Procedure
1. Choose a thin feature with clear business behavior.
2. Define the acceptance evidence before building.
3. Complete analysis, design, code, integration, and tests within the iteration.
4. Demonstrate the running feature.
5. Do not carry hidden test-and-fix work as done.

## Checks
- Done means running and verified, not partially coded.
- The product is releasable at iteration end.
