# KU-0019: Aggregate consistency boundaries

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `design`
**Trigger:** Defining domain objects that carry business rules or deciding transactional scope.

## Purpose
Model transactional consistency around business invariants while keeping change units small and testable.

## Procedure
1. Identify the business invariant that must hold after one operation.
2. Place only the necessary entities and values inside the aggregate boundary.
3. Keep aggregates small and reference other aggregates by identity.
4. Use separate transactions for other aggregates.

## Checks
- Every aggregate has a stated invariant.
- No transaction changes unrelated aggregates without a deliberate consistency decision.
