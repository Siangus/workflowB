# KU-0011: Modular quality

**Type:** `principle`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** Component boundaries or refactor.

## Purpose
Manage coupling, cohesion, hiding, abstraction, and interfaces for changeability.

## Procedure
1. Assign cohesive responsibility.
2. Minimize coupling.
3. Hide details.
4. Define interfaces.
5. Evaluate quality attributes.
