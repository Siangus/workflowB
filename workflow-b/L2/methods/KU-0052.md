# KU-0052: Attribute-Driven Design

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** The system has multiple quality drivers or a nontrivial architecture decision must be made before implementation.

## Purpose
Iteratively derive architecture structures from quality attributes, functionality, constraints, and design concepts.

## Procedure
1. Choose the iteration goal and prioritize drivers.
2. Select the system element to decompose.
3. Identify relevant design concepts and tactics.
4. Instantiate structures and allocate responsibilities.
5. Record preliminary views and analyze the design against the iteration goal.
6. Repeat until risk and scope are sufficient for the next increment.

## Checks
- Each design iteration has a goal and evidence.
- The result addresses the selected drivers.
- Unresolved risks become experiments or decisions.
