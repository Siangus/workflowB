# KU-0048: Performance tactics and resource management

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** The system has throughput, latency, capacity, or resource-use requirements.

## Purpose
Meet timing and throughput goals by managing demand, resources, concurrency, and processing order.

## Procedure
1. Define performance scenarios and workload shape.
2. Identify resource demands and bottlenecks.
3. Choose tactics such as concurrency, scheduling, queueing, caching, replication, resource pooling, or prioritization.
4. Load-test the architecture and compare measured response to targets.

## Checks
- Workload and measurement method are explicit.
- Tests represent peak and representative demand.
- Performance improvements do not violate consistency or security requirements.
