# KU-0051: Architecturally significant requirements

**Type:** `decision_rule`
**Lifecycle:** `requirements`
**Trigger:** A requirement affects performance, availability, security, safety, deployability, modifiability, or system boundaries.

## Purpose
Find the subset of requirements that materially shapes architecture and make their trade-offs explicit.

## Procedure
1. Review business goals, requirements, constraints, and stakeholder concerns.
2. Classify requirements as irrelevant, potentially significant, or clearly significant.
3. Elicit missing scenarios and thresholds.
4. Record the significant requirements in a utility tree or equivalent structure.
5. Use them as drivers for architecture design and evaluation.

## Checks
- Every architecture driver has a source and measurable scenario.
- Architecture review covers the highest-risk drivers.
