# KU-0063: Layering and responsibility boundaries

**Type:** `pattern`
**Lifecycle:** `design`
**Trigger:** An application has multiple kinds of change, complex domain logic, or several clients of the same business behavior.

## Purpose
Separate presentation, domain, and data-source responsibilities to manage coupling while avoiding unnecessary distribution.

## Procedure
1. Identify responsibilities and change forces.
2. Choose logical layers and dependency direction.
3. Keep layer interfaces explicit.
4. Deploy layers separately only when the operational benefit justifies the cost.

## Checks
- Domain rules are not accidentally owned by the UI or persistence layer.
- Distribution is justified by a measurable need.
