# KU-0042: Requirements risk management and process improvement

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `risk`
**Trigger:** A team has recurring requirement failures or starts a project with material uncertainty.

## Purpose
Treat requirement uncertainty, participation gaps, ambiguity, scope growth, and unmanaged change as risks with explicit mitigation and learning.

## Procedure
1. Assess current practice and identify a small number of pain points.
2. Identify requirement-specific risks, probability, impact, triggers, and owners.
3. Select targeted process changes and pilot them.
4. Measure outcomes and adjust incrementally.
5. Escalate unmanaged high-exposure risks.

## Checks
- Risks distinguish future uncertainty from current issues.
- Improvement work has measurable objectives and an owner.
