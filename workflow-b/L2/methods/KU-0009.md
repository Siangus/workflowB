# KU-0009: Feasibility framing

**Type:** `decision_rule`
**Lifecycle:** `problem-framing`
**Trigger:** New product or major shift.

## Purpose
Test business, technical, economic, and operational feasibility.

## Procedure
1. State problem.
2. Measure success.
3. Test viability.
4. Estimate benefit/risk.
5. Choose direction.

## Checks
- Kill criteria exist.
