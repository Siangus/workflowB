# KU-0020: Domain events and eventual consistency

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** A state change must inform another aggregate or bounded context.

## Purpose
Publish business-significant past occurrences to coordinate changes across boundaries without synchronous coupling.

## Procedure
1. Name the event in past tense using ubiquitous language.
2. Include the event's essential business facts and occurrence metadata.
3. Persist the aggregate update and event atomically.
4. Publish through a versioned schema.
5. Make consumers idempotent and handle causal ordering.

## Checks
- Events describe a fact, not a command.
- Consumer retry cannot duplicate a business effect.
- Event ordering requirements are explicit.
