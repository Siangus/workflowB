# KU-0045: Availability and recovery tactics

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** The system must continue or recover after component, network, dependency, or data-store failures.

## Purpose
Design for failure detection, recovery, repair, and controlled loss of service.

## Procedure
1. Classify failure sources and affected services.
2. Define availability scenarios and RTO/RPO expectations.
3. Select tactics such as redundancy, heartbeat, ping/echo, exception handling, rollback, retry, or graceful degradation.
4. Test recovery paths and operational visibility.

## Checks
- Failure modes have detection and response.
- Recovery targets are measurable.
- A failed dependency does not create an uncontrolled cascade.
