# KU-0015: Bounded contexts and ubiquitous language

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** A complex domain contains terms with different meanings across business functions.

## Purpose
Align model, team ownership, code, and terminology within an explicit semantic boundary.

## Procedure
1. Identify competing meanings in business language.
2. Draw a bounded-context boundary around one coherent model.
3. Define the team's ubiquitous language with domain experts.
4. Assign clear ownership and public interfaces.

## Checks
- Each cross-context term has a defined local meaning.
- The boundary has an owning team and published integration contract.
