# KU-0004: Estimation

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `planning`
**Trigger:** Scoping, proposals, or iteration planning.

## Purpose
Reduce uncertainty through decomposition, history, capacity, and confidence ranges.

## Procedure
1. Decompose work.
2. Estimate size.
3. Adjust for productivity and risk.
4. Compare history.
5. Re-estimate at gates.

## Checks
- Range and assumptions explicit.
