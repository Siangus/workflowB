# KU-0054: Architecture documentation, rationale, and debt

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `governance`
**Trigger:** A system has multiple stakeholders, non-obvious constraints, or architecture decisions likely to outlive the current team.

## Purpose
Keep architecture usable and evolvable by documenting views, decisions, rationale, and debt hotspots.

## Procedure
1. Choose views based on stakeholder uses.
2. Document the architecture, interfaces, behavior, and rationale.
3. Relate decisions to requirements and quality scenarios.
4. Inspect the code and dependencies for architecture debt hotspots.
5. Schedule debt reduction with normal delivery work.

## Checks
- A reader can find the view needed for their decision.
- Rationale explains why alternatives were rejected.
- Architecture debt has evidence, owner, and planned treatment.
