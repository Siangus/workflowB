# KU-0038: Just-in-time requirements for agile delivery

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `requirements`
**Trigger:** An exploratory or iterative project uses a product backlog and short delivery cycles.

## Purpose
Keep early agile requirements lightweight while progressively elaborating the next work slice with acceptance evidence.

## Procedure
1. Capture high-level needs as backlog items.
2. Prioritize them by product value and risk.
3. Elaborate the next iteration's stories with the team and user representative.
4. Add acceptance criteria and necessary models before commitment.
5. Retain traceability proportionate to risk.

## Checks
- Near-term stories are understood and testable before implementation.
- Future work remains intentionally less detailed.
