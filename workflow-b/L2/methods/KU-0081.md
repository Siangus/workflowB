# KU-0081: Deployment automation and continuous delivery

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `release`
**Trigger:** A team must release changes regularly or recover from a failed deployment.

## Purpose
Make deployment frequent, repeatable, observable, and reversible to reduce release risk.

## Procedure
1. Package immutable artifacts.
2. Automate environment preparation and verification.
3. Use staged rollout, traffic control, or canary where risk warrants.
4. Separate deployment from activation when useful.
5. Automate rollback or roll-forward and verify the result.

## Checks
- The same process works repeatedly across environments.
- Deployment failure stops or rolls back safely.
- Post-deployment checks verify behavior, not just process completion.
