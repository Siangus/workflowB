# KU-0076: Steady state, back pressure, and load shedding

**Type:** `method`
**Lifecycle:** `architecture`
**Trigger:** Traffic can exceed processing capacity or downstream services can slow.

## Purpose
Keep demand and capacity within safe operating ranges instead of allowing overload to create collapse.

## Procedure
1. Define normal operating rate and safe capacity.
2. Measure queue depth, latency, saturation, and rejection.
3. Apply back pressure to producers or shed lower-value work.
4. Use governors or admission control for expensive operations.
5. Preserve critical traffic while rejecting work explicitly.

## Checks
- Overload produces controlled rejection or delay, not unbounded growth.
- Critical operations retain capacity.
- Users receive an honest status.
