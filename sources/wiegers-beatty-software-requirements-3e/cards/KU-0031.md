## Purpose
Manage policy, constraint, trigger, inference, and calculation rules as reusable business assets rather than copying them into features.

## Trigger
Rules affect multiple requirements, products, or regulatory obligations.

## Procedure
1. Identify the rule and its business source.
2. Classify it and record it atomically with a stable ID.
3. Validate whether it is current and applicable.
4. Trace implementing requirements and tests to the rule ID.
5. Review rule changes for downstream impact.

## Checks
- Rules have owners, sources, and applicability.
- No duplicated rule text silently diverges across specifications.

## Anti-patterns
- Hard-coding volatile policy or combining multiple rules into one opaque statement.
