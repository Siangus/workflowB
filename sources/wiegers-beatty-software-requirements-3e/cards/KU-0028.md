## Purpose
Anchor product decisions in business opportunity, objectives, scope boundaries, and observable success measures.

## Trigger
Starting a product, release, or major enhancement.

## Procedure
1. Identify the business problem or opportunity.
2. Write measurable business objectives and success criteria.
3. Define product vision and the current project or iteration scope.
4. Record exclusions, constraints, stakeholders, and business risks.
5. Use the document to screen proposed scope changes.

## Checks
- Each requested feature can be related to an objective or justified exclusion.
- Vision remains stable while near-term scope can change deliberately.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating a requested solution as a business objective.
