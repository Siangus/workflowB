# KU-0086: Software requirements specification as an implementation-independent baseline

## Purpose

Organize the behavior, qualities, constraints, data, and external-interface needs of one product or increment into a versioned requirements baseline that supports planning, design, construction, testing, and support without dictating an internal solution.

## Trigger

A project needs a technical requirement baseline for an increment, multiple readers need a consistent source of truth, or requirement cards/models are too distributed to serve downstream work.

## Procedure

1. Choose a project-appropriate SRS structure and define version, scope, readers, references, and ID conventions.
2. Organize atomic functional requirements by a reader-appropriate scheme and link their evidence, models, rules, and acceptance scenarios.
3. Specify logical data, external-interface, quality, constraint, assumption, and dependency requirements.
4. Keep requirements traceable, stable-ID based, cross-linked, and free of duplicated content.
5. Baseline each selected increment only after its open issues are resolved, deferred, or explicitly decided by an accountable owner.

## Checks

- The SRS lets developers, testers, planners, and maintainers determine what is required without inferring material behavior.
- Every requirement has a stable ID and does not mix independent behaviors.
- Performance and quality requirements are quantitative or explicitly unresolved decisions.
- Design, implementation, test-plan, and project-plan detail is excluded except for justified constraints.

## Anti-patterns

- One document that combines requirements, architecture, estimates, project plans, quality plans, and tests.
- Treating screenshots or solution choices as a substitute for functional and data requirements.
- Leaving unresolved TBD items for developers to guess during implementation.
