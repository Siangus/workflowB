## Purpose
Choose inter-team and technical integration relationships deliberately rather than allowing accidental coupling.

## Trigger
Two bounded contexts need to exchange behavior or information.

## Procedure
1. Map upstream and downstream contexts.
2. Choose a relationship such as partnership, customer-supplier, shared kernel, conformist, or separate ways.
3. Document ownership, service expectations, and change coordination.
4. Review the relationship as costs and dependencies evolve.

## Checks
- Every integration has an owner, direction, and change policy.
- The mapping explains both team and technical coupling.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating every API edge as a purely technical choice.
