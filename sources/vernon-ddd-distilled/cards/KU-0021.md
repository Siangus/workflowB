## Purpose
Acquire domain knowledge quickly, turn uncertainty into tested scenarios, and retain unfinished modeling work as visible debt.

## Trigger
Starting a complex domain, facing ambiguous workflows, or discovering a model mismatch during an iteration.

## Procedure
1. Run a time-boxed event-storming session with domain experts and engineers.
2. Identify events, commands, aggregates, policies, and unanswered questions.
3. Refine high-risk scenarios with domain experts.
4. Create acceptance tests or executable specifications.
5. Record unresolved model work as modeling debt and schedule it.

## Checks
- Domain experts review critical scenarios and acceptance tests.
- Unresolved model questions have an owner and backlog item.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating task-board movement as sufficient design discovery.
