## Purpose
Describe different architectural concerns with a viewtype whose elements, relations, properties, and notation are explicit.

## Trigger
A stakeholder needs to understand a structural, runtime, deployment, implementation, or work-assignment concern.

## Procedure
1. Identify the concern and intended use.
2. Choose module, component-and-connector, or allocation viewtype.
3. Define elements, relations, properties, and notation.
4. Connect the view to supporting catalogs and other views.

## Checks
- The view answers a named stakeholder question.
- Every symbol and relation has a defined meaning.

## Anti-patterns
- Using one overloaded diagram for every concern.
- Mixing logical layers, runtime tiers, and deployment nodes without a mapping.
