Establish controlled communication, planning, modeling, construction, deployment, and umbrella activities.

Trigger: Starting any nontrivial product or process improvement.

Inputs
- Business need
- Stakeholders
- Constraints

Outputs
- Process map
- Umbrella activity plan

Procedure
1. Define framework activities.
2. Select a process flow.
3. Attach quality, configuration, measurement, and risk activities.
4. Review fit each iteration.

Checks
- Every activity has owner and artifact.
- Deviations are recorded.

Anti-patterns
- Adopting heavy process under high uncertainty.
