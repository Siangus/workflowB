Test business, technical, economic, and operational feasibility.

Trigger: New product or major shift.

Inputs
- Objective
- Concept
- Constraints

Outputs
- Proceed/pivot/stop
- Success criteria

Procedure
1. State problem.
2. Measure success.
3. Test viability.
4. Estimate benefit/risk.
5. Choose direction.

Checks
- Kill criteria exist.

Anti-patterns
- Solution before feasibility.
