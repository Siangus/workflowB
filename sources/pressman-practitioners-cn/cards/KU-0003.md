Align goals, vocabulary, risks, and decision authority.

Trigger: Cross-functional work or unclear ownership.

Inputs
- Problem statement
- Stakeholders

Outputs
- Communication plan
- Decision register

Procedure
1. Identify stakeholders.
2. Name decision owners.
3. Select channels.
4. Record assumptions and issues.

Checks
- No decision lacks an owner.

Anti-patterns
- Meetings replacing written decisions.
