## Purpose
Steer scope at a short cadence instead of pretending a distant detailed plan is reliable.

## Trigger
Iterative work with uncertainty, changing evidence, or a fixed time and budget envelope.

## Procedure
1. Set the time and funding boundary.
2. Keep a prioritized feature backlog.
3. Split stories into small business-recognizable slices.
4. Let the team select feasible work using recent delivery evidence.
5. Replan before each iteration and remove excess work early.

## Checks
- The iteration commitment is based on evidence, not stretch pressure.
- Deferred work remains visible without being treated as failure.

## Anti-patterns
- Detailed long-range task plans treated as commitments; stretch goals that trade away quality.
